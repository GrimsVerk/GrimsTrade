# multi-signal: weekly cross-sectional rank IC (train, Fridays 1994-02-04 .. 2025-11)

Card `cards/multi-signal.md`; script `lib/multisignal_ic.py`. Descriptive screen, no
backtest. Placebo A: scores shuffled among each week's assets (2,000 draws); placebo B:
every fixed relabelling of the assets (719 for six, 23 for the ETFs). Verdict = the card's
four-part bar (1 partial IC > 0 at 99.5%; 2 placebos; 3 ETF sign and eras; 4 size 0.10).
