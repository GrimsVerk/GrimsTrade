### The mechanisms (cause story each)

- **P1 `arms-fear`** (criminology / firearm-policy research). FBI NICS background checks for
  gun sales surge when households are afraid (unrest, pandemic, expected gun laws after
  elections and mass shootings; Depetris-Chauvin 2015 `[memory]`). A costly household act,
  not a price or a survey answer. If that fear is shared by the marginal investor and priced,
  the US market earns more afterwards.
- **P2 `approval-mood`** (public-opinion research). Gallup presidential approval as a measure
  of national mood (Stimson's public mood; Nofsinger 2005 "social mood" `[memory]`).
- **P3 `declaration-politics`** (emergency management). FEMA major-disaster declarations are
  political as well as physical (more in election years, Garrett and Sobel 2003; Reeves 2011
  `[memory]`): a fiscal transfer flow.
- **P4 `road-deaths`** (road-safety epidemiology). Monthly US traffic fatalities (FARS) as a
  real-time trace of driving and drinking, hence consumption.
- **P5 `firm-births`** (entrepreneurship). Census Business Formation Statistics: surges in
  new-business applications as entrepreneurs' optimism (over-entry, low later returns).
- **P6 `box-office`** (cinema). Weekend box office of escapist versus dark films as mood.
- **P7 `chart-valence`** (musicology). Valence of the songs people stream (Edmans,
  Fernandez-Perez, Garel and Indriawan, JFE 2022 `[memory]`).
- **P8 `trade-remedy`** (trade law). Antidumping petitions protect the petitioning industry
  (Hughes, Lenway and Rayburn 1997 `[memory]`).
- **P9 `conclave`** (religion). Papal deaths and conclaves as a mood event in Catholic
  countries.
- **P10 `exam-season`** (education). National exam and school-term calendars as a
  household-attention cycle.
- **P11 `earth-rotation`** (geodesy). Length-of-day anomalies (IERS).
- **P12 `newspaper-strike`** (media studies). Newspaper strikes cut the flow of news (Peress,
  JF 2014 `[memory]`: trading and volatility fall).
- **P13 `benford-drift`** (forensic statistics). Departures from Benford's law in aggregate
  reported numbers as a sign of reporting stress.
