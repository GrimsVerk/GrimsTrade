### The mechanisms (cause story each)

- **P1 `arms-fear`** (criminology / firearm-policy research). FBI NICS background checks for
  gun sales surge when households are afraid (unrest, pandemic, expected gun laws after
  elections and mass shootings; Depetris-Chauvin 2015 `[memory]`). A costly household act,
  not a price or a survey answer. If that fear is shared by the marginal investor and priced,
  the US market earns more afterwards.
- **P2 `approval-mood`** (public-opinion research). Gallup presidential approval as a measure
  of national mood (Stimson's public mood; Nofsinger 2005 "social mood" `[memory]`).
- **P3 `declaration-politics`** (emergency management). FEMA major-disaster declarations are
  political as well as physical (more in election years, Garrett and Sobel 2003; Reeves 2011
  `[memory]`): a fiscal transfer flow.
- **P4 `road-deaths`** (road-safety epidemiology). Monthly US traffic fatalities (FARS) as a
  real-time trace of driving and drinking, hence consumption.
- **P5 `firm-births`** (entrepreneurship). Census Business Formation Statistics: surges in
  new-business applications as entrepreneurs' optimism (over-entry, low later returns).
- **P6 `box-office`** (cinema). Weekend box office of escapist versus dark films as mood.
- **P7 `chart-valence`** (musicology). Valence of the songs people stream (Edmans,
  Fernandez-Perez, Garel and Indriawan, JFE 2022 `[memory]`).
- **P8 `trade-remedy`** (trade law). Antidumping petitions protect the petitioning industry
  (Hughes, Lenway and Rayburn 1997 `[memory]`).
- **P9 `conclave`** (religion). Papal deaths and conclaves as a mood event in Catholic
  countries.
- **P10 `exam-season`** (education). National exam and school-term calendars as a
  household-attention cycle.
- **P11 `earth-rotation`** (geodesy). Length-of-day anomalies (IERS).
- **P12 `newspaper-strike`** (media studies). Newspaper strikes cut the flow of news (Peress,
  JF 2014 `[memory]`: trading and volatility fall).
- **P13 `benford-drift`** (forensic statistics). Departures from Benford's law in aggregate
  reported numbers as a sign of reporting stress.

## What came out (one real run, 2026-11-26 00:08 UTC)

### W45 `arms-fear`: passes the kill-only gate, killed by its replication R45

**In the three months after a surge in gun buying the US market earned more: b +10.18% per
unit of the dose (+1.82% per SD of the dose, SD 0.179), t +3.50, 182 months 2008-2023; the
true t ranks 2 of 41 among earlier-dose calendars and 2 of 41 among IAAFT surrogates. That is
the third kill-only pass of the family (after W32 and W36). The replication R45, fixed in the
card, then fails: rows 2000-01 .. 2008-05, b +4.95% per unit (+0.89% per SD), t +0.54, rank 13
of 41 among the gate's IAAFT surrogates (17 of 41 among phase surrogates, the family the
card's R45 text names). Dead: no card for a strategy.** Held-out data untouched.

- **The pass rests on few episodes.** The first half (2008-2015, with the late-2008 and
  early-2013 surges) gives t +1.16; the second half gives +3.21, and dropping the 17 pandemic
  months (2020-02 .. 2021-06: the spring-2020 buying surge, then the rebound) takes t to
  +1.95. The controls change little (no controls +4.13): the market's own past return and
  variance do not absorb it.
- **The nulls are wide.** Earlier-dose calendars: SD 1.95, 40% past |t| 2, 5/50/95% -2.34 /
  +0.30 / +3.19, extremes -3.35 / +3.71 (k 53). The shifted t move in a wave across k (k 25-31
  near -2, k 49-55 +2.6 to +3.7, k 73-81 near -2 to -3.4, k 91-99 near +3): the dose has a
  strong cycle of a few years (election and policy cycles in gun buying: a guess, not tested), and shifting it lines
  it up with the market's own swings. IAAFT surrogates: SD 1.52, 23% past 2, 5/50/95% -2.05 /
  +0.53 / +2.79, max +3.50. A null this wide is what gate-null.md's rank rule is for: a plain
  t +3.50 would have looked far stronger than it is. IAAFT was the stricter choice: 40 phase
  surrogates (seed 2745, checked once after the review, 2026-11-26) rank the gate's t 1 of 41.
- **The replication.** R45's 101 months carry the 2000-2008 cycle (gun sales rose through the
  2000s, the 2003-2007 bull market): b keeps the sign at half the size and t +0.54. With SE(b)
  about 9% per unit there, R45 is weak (the card said so); it cannot confirm the gate, and by
  the fixed rule it kills it, as R32 and R36 did before.
- **Event dates.** Row m enters at the end of month m+1, after the FBI's table for month m is
  out (first days of m+1 `[memory]`); the outcome starts at m+2. The CSV stops at 2023-09
  (BuzzFeed News stopped updating it), so the train window's last 25 months (2023-10 ..
  2025-10) were not used; they sit in the FBI's own monthly PDF, not fetched tonight. The
  publication lag (also in 2000-2008) and the "not revised" claim are from memory, untested,
  and the CSV is a 2023 vintage of a cumulative PDF; the verdict is unaffected, since R45
  failed.
- My guess (b slightly positive, t between -1 and +1.5, middle ranks; pass 3%) was wrong on the
  gate and right on the replication.

Label: **kill-only pass, killed by R45** (gate-null step 7: smallest p 1/41 = 0.024, above the
family bar and the register's 9.8e-5).
