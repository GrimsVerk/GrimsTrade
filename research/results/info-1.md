Script `lib/info1_ic.py`, card `research/info-1.md`. IC = Pearson correlation with the
coming week's risk-adjusted return (`rl3_plant.week_z`). Chance = 95th percentile of
|IC| with the feature's weekly series circularly shifted (500 shifts of >= 52
weeks). WF = walk-forward IC (OLS refitted every two years, a year's gap); `per` = a
slope per asset, `pool` = one slope. Gain = WF IC of trend inputs + feature minus trend
inputs alone, same rows. Overlap = largest |corr| with the 7 trend inputs.
