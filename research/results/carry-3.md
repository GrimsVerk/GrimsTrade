Script `lib/carry3.py`; card and reading in `research/carry-3.md`. Rows without a suffix are at the realistic cost: taker fee + modelled half-spread + square-root impact for a 10,000 USD account, smallest order 10 USD, plus every funding event. `nocost`: no trading cost; `flat10`/`flat30`: carry-2's flat 10/30 bps a side; `x2`: spread and impact doubled; `A100k`/`A1M`: account 100,000 / 1,000,000 USD; `maker`: 60% of each trade at the maker fee without spread; `top50`: only the 50 highest-ADV tradable coins per venue. `Turn/yr`: traded value per year in multiples of the account. `Cost/side`: average cost per unit traded. `Fee/yr`, `Spread/yr`, `Impact/yr`, `Funding/yr`: share of equity per year. `Gross`: mean gross exposure. `p(S>0)`: one-sided stationary-bootstrap p.
