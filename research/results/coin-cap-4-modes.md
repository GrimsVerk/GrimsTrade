# coin-cap-4, step 1: why the weekly coin stop fails in fast crashes and on BTC

Generated by `research/lib/coin_cap4_modes.py` (descriptive, post-hoc by nature, nothing logged). Same book, weekly twin `TS`, weekly `stop-0.25`, 445 coin falls and 21 episodes as `results/coin-cap-2.md`; matched cap `f*` 0.663. Prices as change from the top's close; days from the top. Band: the stop's exit level in price terms, `exp(-0.25 x vol) - 1`, vol = its own 60-day EWMA (annualised). Exit: the last day the stop earns after the top. After exit: the stop's summed slot return from the first exit to the window's end (re-entries only; negative = re-entry losses). Where M sits: the stop's deepest P&L fall ends before its first exit (`first leg`), after a re-entry (`re-entry`), or does not start at the top (`other`).
